The Real Test 📋

Ealing Fields, Ada Lovelace and Twyford all use the same format: 60 questions, 4 sections, 1 hour.

🎯
Pitch
Can you tell if the second note is higher or lower than the first?
🎶
Melody
You hear a short tune twice. Did anything change? Which note was different?
🥁
Rhythm
You hear a rhythm twice. Same or different? Which beat changed?
🎸
Texture
How many instruments are playing? Did the number change?

💡 The test measures what you naturally hear, not what you've been taught. Playing music helps because it trains your ear without you realising.
